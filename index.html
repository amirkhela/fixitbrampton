What We Do

Everything on the honey-do list.

From the wobbling IKEA dresser to the fence panel that fell over in the wind — we handle the small-and-medium jobs most contractors won't take the time for.

Furniture Assembly

IKEA, Wayfair, Amazon, cribs, bed frames — flat-pack everything built right, with every screw where it belongs.

Drywall & Painting

Doorknob holes, hairline cracks, ceiling patches and full-room repaints. Sanded smooth, colour-matched, no mess left behind.

Plumbing (Minor)

Dripping taps, running toilets, shower heads, garburators, and drain snaking. If it's bigger than a seal, we'll say so up front.

Electrical (Minor)

Light fixtures, ceiling fans, dimmers, outlets and smart switches. Proper ESA-compliant work for the simple stuff.

Doors & Windows

Sticking doors planed, broken handles replaced, weather stripping, screens re-meshed and closers tuned.

Decks & Fences

Loose boards, soft spots, wobbly rails, fence posts concreted back in, gates re-hung, stain and seal before winter.

TV & Art Mounting

Any TV size wall-mounted, stud-anchored, cables hidden and levelled to the millimetre. Picture galleries too.

Shelving & Closets

Floating shelves, pantry systems, custom closet organizers and garage wall storage — anchored into studs, level, square.

Caulk & Weatherproof

Bathtub, shower and kitchen re-caulked. Window sashes and door frames sealed so your furnace isn't heating the street.

General Repairs

Door hinges, loose railings, squeaky stairs, fallen curtain rods — all the little things on the list you keep meaning to fix.

Yard & Cleanup

Spring and fall cleanup, garden bed prep, small tree trimming, junk hauling, and shed organization.

Frequently Asked

Before you text.

Short answers to what most people ask when they're about to reach out. Still not sure? Just text (289) 275-3973 — we'll tell you straight.

Do you actually speak Punjabi?

Yes. Fix It Brampton is Punjabi-owned. Call or text in Punjabi, Hindi or English — whichever is easier for you. Many of our clients are Punjabi aunties and uncles who'd rather describe a problem in their own language than translate on the fly.

ਸਾਡੇ ਨਾਲ ਆਪਣੀ ਭਾਸ਼ਾ ਵਿੱਚ ਗੱਲ ਕਰੋ — ਇਹ ਸਾਡੇ ਲਈ ਵੀ ਸੌਖਾ ਹੈ।
How much does a handyman cost in Brampton?

Most small jobs — TV mounting, furniture assembly, a leaky faucet, a couple of shelves — land between $80 and $180. Bigger work (drywall repair, fence boards, full-room paint) gets a fixed written quote after a look or a few texted photos. Either way, you see the number before we start.

How quickly can you come?

We reply to every text within a few hours between 8am and 6pm, seven days a week. Small jobs are usually booked for later that week; urgent leaks or safety issues we try to handle same-day when our schedule allows.

Do you bring your own tools and materials?

All standard hand tools, cordless drivers, ladders, drywall supplies and painting gear — yes, always. Specific materials (a particular paint colour, a mount bracket, a faucet) we either pick up with receipts or you can buy ahead of time. Whatever's easier.

Can I pay by e-Transfer?

Yes — cash or Interac e-Transfer. You get a proper written invoice either way. No HST surprise on jobs below the small-supplier threshold.

What if the fix doesn't hold?

We stand behind our work for a week after the job. If something we fixed is acting up, text us — we come back and make it right, no second bill.

Is there anything you won't do?

Major electrical panel work, furnace and AC installs, gas lines, roofing, and structural stuff — those need a licensed specialist and we'll tell you so on the first text. We'd rather send you to the right trade than do something we shouldn't.
