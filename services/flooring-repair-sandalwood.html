Flooring Repair FAQ · Sandalwood

Before you text.

Can you fix just one laminate board?

If you have a spare, yes — $120–180 including the labour to pop the row, swap the board, and re-lock. If we source the match, add the board cost.

How much does tile repair cost?

Single cracked tile replacement $160–240 including grout. Large area (6+ tiles from one water event) $400–600. Matching the tile depends on supply.

Can you fix squeaky floors?

Often yes — from above if the floor is screw-friendly, from below if the basement ceiling is open. Quiet fixes without floor damage.
