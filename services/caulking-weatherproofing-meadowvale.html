Caulking & Weatherproofing FAQ · Meadowvale

Before you text.

How much does re-caulking cost?

Single bathtub/shower surround $140–180. Kitchen sink and backsplash $95–130. Window and door weatherproofing $120–200 per door/window pair.

Why does caulk fail so fast?

Usually wrong product. Bathroom needs 100% silicone, not latex. Exterior uses urethane or polyurethane, not silicone. We use the right product for the job.

Can you do exterior caulking too?

Yes — window frames, door trim, dryer vents. Done in dry weather above 5°C so it cures properly.
