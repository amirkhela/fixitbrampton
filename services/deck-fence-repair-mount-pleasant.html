Deck & Fence Repair FAQ · Mount Pleasant

Before you text.

How much does deck repair cost?

Single loose board $120–180. Stair tread $160 each. Railing re-secure $150. Soft-spot repair (3–5 boards) $280–450. Full re-stain from $450.

Can you reset a leaning fence post?

Yes — rotten posts get sistered or replaced; wind-shifted posts get re-plumbed, braced, and concrete-set. Flat $240 per post including lumber and concrete.

When is the best time to stain a deck?

Late spring through early fall — above 10°C, no rain 48 hours either side. In the GTA that's May through October.
