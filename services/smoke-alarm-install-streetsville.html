Smoke Alarm & CO Install FAQ · Streetsville

Before you text.

How many smoke alarms do I need in Ontario?

One on every floor (including finished basement), plus one outside each sleeping area. A 2-storey home with a basement typically needs 4–5 smoke alarms plus 1–2 CO alarms.

Can you install hardwired alarms?

Yes, as direct replacements on existing boxes. New-circuit hardwiring requires a licensed electrician — we'll refer. Most homeowners are fine with 10-year sealed battery models anyway.

Are you licensed for this?

Installing smoke and CO alarms doesn't require a license — it's a homeowner-permitted task. We follow TSSA and Fire Code placement rules carefully.
