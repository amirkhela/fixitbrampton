Pressure Washing FAQ · Fletcher's Meadow

Before you text.

How much does pressure washing cost?

Standard driveway $180–240. Driveway + walkway + front porch $260–320. Add back deck $100–140. Full property package (driveway, deck, siding, fence) $380–480.

Will pressure washing damage my interlock?

Not when done at the right PSI (2000–2800 on interlock) with the right tip. Too-close contact or a 0° tip will etch — we don't use either. Re-sanding joints available add-on.

Can you do this in spring for deck prep?

Yes — most of our deck-staining jobs start with a power wash 24–48 hours before. Book both in one call for a combined rate.
