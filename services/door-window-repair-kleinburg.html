Full exterior door replacement with a new frame is a 2-person, half-day job — we do it, but quote on-site. Swapping an existing door into existing hinges/hardware is a same-visit job.

How much does it cost to fix a sticking door?

Plane, rehang, and adjust a single interior door runs $95–140. If the jamb has shifted and needs shimming, $160–220.

Yes — window and patio door screens, including pet-resistant mesh. Per-screen price $45–85 depending on size.
