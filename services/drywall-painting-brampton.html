What's Included

Patches that actually disappear.

The cheap way is to slap mud on, sand once, and hope. The right way is a mesh or California patch for anything bigger than a nail, three coats of compound with feathered edges, proper priming, and a paint match that doesn't "ghost" under side-lighting.

  • Nail holes, screw holes, anchor holes — filled, sanded, primed, painted
  • Doorknob holes repaired with a proper patch (not just mud — that cracks within a year)
  • Fist-sized to furniture-sized holes: mesh or California patch, three-coat mudding, feathered sanding
  • Hairline settling cracks: reinforced with paper tape, compound, primer, paint
  • Popcorn ceiling patches matched with texture spray for ceiling repairs
  • Paint colour-matched from your can or scan-matched at the store if the can is gone
  • Full interior rooms: walls, trim, ceilings — cut in by hand, rolled with premium Benjamin Moore / Sherwin-Williams
  • Drop cloths, plastic sheeting, vacuum-assisted sanding, shop-vac cleanup — zero dust on your couch

Drywall & Painting FAQ

Before you text.

How much does drywall repair cost in Brampton?

Small patches (doorknob holes, nail holes, picture-hanger holes) bundled together start at $85 for up to five in the same room. A fist-size hole with matching paint runs $120–160. Larger patches (moving-day damage, water damage prep) run $180–280 depending on size and paint match. Full room repaint starts at $450 for a standard bedroom.

ਬਰੈਂਪਟਨ ਵਿੱਚ ਡਰਾਈਵਾਲ ਮੁਰੰਮਤ $85 ਤੋਂ ਸ਼ੁਰੂ।
Can you match my existing paint colour?

If you have the original can, we use it. If not, we take a chip to the paint store and scan-match. On modern latex, the match is indistinguishable once dry. If the wall has faded from sunlight, we recommend painting the whole wall corner-to-corner for a perfect blend instead of trying to spot-patch.

Do you control the dust?

Yes. Drop cloths on floors, plastic sheeting over nearby furniture, a vacuum-assisted sander on big patches, and a shop-vac pass before we leave. Nothing tracks into the rest of the house.

How long does it take to dry?

Small patches dry in 60–90 minutes and we paint them the same visit. Larger patches need overnight drying for the compound, so it's usually a two-visit job — one to patch, one to paint the next day.

Do you paint ceilings too?

Flat and stippled ceilings, yes. Smooth ceilings get rolled with flat ceiling paint. Popcorn ceilings we patch with texture spray to match the existing pattern — we don't try to paint those flat, it never looks right.
