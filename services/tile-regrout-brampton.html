Tile Regrout & Repair FAQ · Brampton

Before you text.

How much does regrouting a shower cost?

Standard tub surround regrout $240–340. Walk-in shower $340–420. Kitchen backsplash $180–280. Loose or cracked tile add-on $40–60 per tile.

Is regrouting a good idea vs full replacement?

Almost always yes — if the tiles themselves are sound and only the grout is damaged, regrouting saves 80–90% vs a full re-tile and lasts 8–10 years.

Will the new grout match my old tile?

We bring colour samples. Most cement-based grout is available in 30+ shades; stone tile sometimes needs unsanded. Colour match is usually within one shade of original.
