TV Mounting FAQ

Before you text.

How much does it cost to mount a TV in Brampton?

Standard wall mounting runs $130 for TVs up to 55 inches, $160 for 56–75 inch TVs, and $200–220 for 76 inches and up. Hiding cables in the wall adds $40–60 depending on drywall conditions. Bracket included if you don't have one. No separate trip fee for Brampton, Mississauga, Caledon, or Bolton.

Can you mount on brick or concrete walls?

Yes. We bring masonry drill bits and anchors rated well above your TV's weight. Brick and concrete hold heavier TVs more securely than drywall — it just takes the right hardware and a slower drilling pace to keep the wall from cracking.

Can you hide the cables inside the wall?

On standard drywall with no fire-blocking behind it, yes — we cut two small openings, fish the HDMI cable down to a low-voltage bracket, install an in-wall Code-compliant power kit, and the wall comes out looking built-in. On exterior walls or older homes with horizontal blocking, we use a paintable surface raceway that disappears once painted.

How long does the install take?

Basic install is 45–60 minutes. Adding cable hiding, a soundbar, or a streaming box brings it closer to 90 minutes. We bring drop cloths and a shop vac — the floor is clean before we leave.

Do you move the existing TV/mount to a new wall?

Yes. We remove the old mount, patch and sand the drywall holes flush, and reinstall on the new wall. The patched side looks untouched once it's painted — which we can do on the same visit if you have a paint match.
