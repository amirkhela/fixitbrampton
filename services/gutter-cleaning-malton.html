How much does gutter cleaning cost in the GTA?

Bungalow flat-rate $140–160. 2-storey detached $180–220. 3-storey or long-run homes $220–260. Single flat rate — no per-linear-foot math.

How often should gutters be cleaned?

Twice a year for most GTA homes — once in late spring after the maple keys drop, once in late fall after the leaves. Homes with overhanging trees may need 3×.

Can you install gutter guards?

Yes — we install snap-in mesh guards on existing gutters. Aluminum screens $8–12/linear foot installed. We prefer mesh over foam or bristle — less clogging.
