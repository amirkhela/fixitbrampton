Can you haul the old appliance away?

Yes — either to your curb for municipal pickup (free) or hauled to a depot for $40–60 depending on the unit.

Do you handle gas appliances?

Yes for connections that use a shut-off valve and certified flex line. New gas line installs require a licensed gas fitter — we'll refer.
