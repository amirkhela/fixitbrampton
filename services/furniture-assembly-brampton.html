Furniture Assembly FAQ

Before you text.

How much does furniture assembly cost in Brampton?

Small pieces (nightstands, small desks, a PAX wardrobe with 2 doors) start at $75. Mid-size pieces (dressers, queen bed frames, bookcases) run $120–160. Full bedroom sets, large KALLAX wall units, or PAX closets with multiple doors come in at $180–280. You see the flat quote before we start — no hourly surprises.

Will you anchor the furniture to the wall?

Yes — always, and it's free. Tall dressers and bookcases get an anti-tip strap drilled into a stud. This matters a lot if you have toddlers, but honestly every tall piece should be anchored — IKEA ships the strap for a reason.

Can you take the cardboard away?

Flattened to your curb, yes — included in the price if your recycling day is in the next three days. If it's further out and you don't have room to store it, we can haul it for $25–40 depending on volume.

What if a piece is missing from the box?

We photograph the issue, pause the build, and help you open a replacement ticket with IKEA or the retailer on the spot. Once the part arrives, we come back to finish — no extra trip charge.

Can you pick the furniture up from the store?

For Brampton and Mississauga IKEA pickups, yes — we'll add the pickup fee and trip to the quote. Usually faster and cheaper than a delivery slot.
