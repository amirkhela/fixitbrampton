What's Included

Fixed properly, not patched over.

Pressure-treated lumber only. Concrete for post resets. Galvanized or stainless fasteners so they don't rust out in two years. Stain and sealant we'd put on our own homes.

  • Loose or soft deck boards pulled, joists inspected for rot, replaced with matching pressure-treated lumber

  • All waste hauled — no rotten wood or broken concrete chunks left on your lawn

Deck & Fence FAQ

Before you text.

How much does deck repair cost in Brampton?

Replacing a single loose deck board runs $120–180 including lumber. Full stair-tread replacement $160 each. Loose railing tightened and re-secured $150. A soft-spot repair covering 3–5 boards $280–450. Full deck re-staining from $450 for a small 10×10 up to $1,200 for a wraparound — that includes Olympic Maximum or Behr Premium stain.

ਬਰੈਂਪਟਨ ਵਿੱਚ ਡੈੱਕ ਮੁਰੰਮਤ $120 ਤੋਂ ਸ਼ੁਰੂ।
Can you reset a leaning fence post?

Yes. Rotten at the base — we either sister a pressure-treated 4×4 beside the stump or dig the stump out and pour a fresh concrete footing with a new post. Wind-shifted but still solid — we re-plumb, brace, and pour fresh concrete. Flat $240 per post including lumber, concrete, and hauling the old post/concrete away.

Do you do full fence replacements?

Individual panel and post replacement, yes. Full fence rebuilds (40+ feet of new fence) are bigger than a handyman job — we'll tell you so up front and refer you to a fence contractor we trust rather than overreach on a job that needs a crew.

When should I stain my deck?

Late spring through early fall. Above 10°C, no rain for 48 hours before or after. In Brampton, that's May through October. Power wash first, let it dry 24–48 hours, then two coats of quality stain. We handle every step on the same job.

Can you do gate repairs too?

Yes — dragging gates, bent hinges, latches that don't close, posts that leaned enough to throw the gate out of square. Most gate fixes are $150–220 including new hardware.
