Shelving & Closets FAQ · Huttonville

Before you text.

Can you do a full PAX closet?

Yes — from flat-pack through install and anchoring. Pricing depends on the number of frames and doors; 2-frame basic $240, 4-frame with doors $380–480.

Will the shelves actually hold weight?

Yes — we anchor into studs, not drywall anchors, for anything over 10 lbs. A 36-inch floating shelf anchored properly holds 80+ lbs evenly loaded.

Can you build custom shelving?

Simple cleat and plywood built-ins, yes. Full-cabinetry style built-ins (with face frames and doors) is carpenter work — we refer out.
